In this tutorial, we will transfer assets privately between two different wallets: your [Bread Wallet](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/bread-wallet-by-miden/coajhopfooegmaifelglfboehacldcbo) and a [Para Wallet](https://www.getpara.com/), an embedded wallet you log into with your email address.

On Miden, every Account is either public or private, a choice encoded in its [Account ID](https://docs.miden.xyz/reference/protocol/account/id#account-type):

- **Public Accounts**: the account state (code, storage and vault) is stored on-chain, so anyone can see its balance and activity, much like an account on most blockchains.
- **Private Accounts**: only a commitment (hash) to the account state is stored on-chain. The state itself stays on your device, so your balance and activity remain private to you.

The Bread Wallet used in this tutorial must be a **Private** account. The Para Wallet that we will create later in this tutorial is a **Public** account.

Make sure the Bread Wallet extension is pinned in your browser, then click the _"Select Wallet"_ button, select _"Bread Wallet"_ and accept the connection request in Bread Wallet.

The Playground automatically imports your wallet. Once it appears on the accounts page, {props.wallet ? <Link href={`/accounts/${props.wallet.identifier}`}>click its name</Link> : 'click its name'} to inspect it and proceed to the next step.
