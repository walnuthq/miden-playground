In the previous step, we requested 100 tokens from the [Miden Faucet](https://faucet.testnet.miden.io/).

The resulting public note containing our tokens must now be consumed to fund our wallet privately.

Click on the <EllipsisVertical className="size-4 inline" /> icon button in the _"Consumable Notes"_ table to consume the note with your wallet.
