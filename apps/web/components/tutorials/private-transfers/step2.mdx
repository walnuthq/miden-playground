Now that your **Private** wallet is connected, let's fund it by requesting tokens from the **Public** [Miden Faucet](https://faucet.testnet.miden.io/).

The Miden Faucet only creates **Public** notes: the funding note, including its recipient and the tokens it carries, is stored on-chain.
Your wallet itself remains **Private**: its state stays off-chain and only its commitment is stored publicly.

Click on the _"Mint"_ button to automatically request 100 tokens from the Miden Faucet.

<MintButton />

Once the mint request is completed, let's wait a couple more seconds to make sure the output note generated by the Miden Faucet has been committed to testnet network.

Notice how the note containing your requested tokens automatically appears under the _"Consumable Notes"_ section of your connected wallet.
