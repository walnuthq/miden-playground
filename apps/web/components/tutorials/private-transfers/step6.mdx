Congratulations! You have used one of Miden's most exciting features: privacy-preserving transfers.

Here is what the outside world can see of what you did:

- **Bread Wallet**: as a **Private** account, only a commitment to its state is stored on-chain, so its balance and activity stay private to you. Only the public note sent by the USDCx Faucet revealed the tokens it received.
- **The transfer**: the private note sent to your Para Wallet only exposes its hash and metadata on-chain, while the assets it carries and its target account stay off-chain. When the Para Wallet consumed it, the note's [nullifier](https://docs.miden.xyz/builder/glossary/#nullifier) marked it as spent without revealing which note it was.
- **Para Wallet**: as a **Public** account, its state is stored on-chain, so anyone can see its balance and Transactions.

Open the Bread Wallet browser extension, or your Para Wallet details page in the Playground, to review your activity and see all the operations you performed during this tutorial.
