Now let's send some tokens from your Bread Wallet to your Para Wallet. The send transaction must be signed by the sender, so you first need to switch back to Bread Wallet.

To disconnect Para Wallet, click the Para button showing your wallet address (_"0x…"_) in the top-right corner to open the Para modal. Click the profile icon button, the last of the four buttons below your balance, then click _"Disconnect"_ at the bottom of the _"Profile"_ view. Your Para Wallet stays listed on the accounts page.

Then click the _"Select Wallet"_ button, select _"Bread Wallet"_ and accept the connection request to reconnect it.

On the <Link href="/accounts">accounts page</Link>, click the <EllipsisVertical className="size-4 inline" /> icon button in your Bread Wallet's row and select the _"New send transaction"_ option to open the configuration modal.

Select **Para Wallet** as the target account and **USDCX** as the asset, then enter an amount of **at least 1**. USDCx is also the asset used to pay transaction fees on testnet: your Para Wallet starts empty, so it pays the fee of the consume transaction in the next step with the tokens you send it. Don't send much more than that either, as your Bread Wallet needs to keep enough USDCx to cover its own fees. Leave the note type set to **Private**.

Click the _"Preview"_ button: Bread Wallet will ask you to confirm the send transaction, which creates a private P2ID output note carrying your tokens that only the Para Wallet can consume.

For a private note, only its hash and its metadata, such as its sender, are stored on-chain: the note details, including the assets it carries and its target account, stay off-chain. To let the Para Wallet discover and consume it, the encrypted note details are sent through the [Note Transport Layer](https://docs.miden.xyz/reference/protocol/account/address#communicating-channel-information), where the recipient can fetch them.
