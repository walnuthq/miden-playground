Now that you understand how the **Count Reader** contract works, let's deploy it on testnet.

Head to the <Link href="/accounts">accounts page</Link>, click the _"Create new account"_ button at the top of the page and select the _"Deploy account"_ option.

Enter a name for your contract and leave the storage mode set to **Public**. Keep **No Auth** as the authentication component: it doesn't require any signature, so you can invoke the contract's procedures without a wallet. Then select the **Count Reader** account component.

Once the component is selected, its **`count_reader::count_reader::count`** storage slot is listed with its default value: keep the initial count set to **0**.

Finally, click the _"Deploy"_ button. This takes a few moments: the Playground creates the Account, then funds it so that it can pay transaction fees. Funding the Account is its first Transaction, which also registers it on testnet.

Once your **Count Reader** is deployed, {props.countReader ? <Link href={`/accounts/${props.countReader.identifier}`}>click its name</Link> : 'click its name'} on the accounts page to open its details page.
