The diagram above shows the **Count Reader** smart contract using FPI to read the count of a counter contract. After reading it, the **Count Reader** writes the value returned by the counter contract to its own storage.

What you'll cover:

- Deploying a **Count Reader** smart contract.
- Executing **cross-contract calls** in the browser.
