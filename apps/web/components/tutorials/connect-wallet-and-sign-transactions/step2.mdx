Now that your wallet is connected, let's fund it by requesting tokens from the public [USDCx Faucet](https://faucet.testnet.miden.io/).

Click the _"Mint"_ button to request tokens from the faucet. They are sent to your wallet in a public note.

<MintButton />

Once the request completes, wait a few more seconds for the note sent by the USDCx Faucet to be committed on testnet.

The note containing your tokens then automatically appears in the _"Consumable Notes"_ section of your wallet.

By default, Bread Wallet automatically consumes the notes it receives, so unless you disabled this option, the note should disappear a few seconds later, once it has been consumed.

If you disabled auto-consume, click the <EllipsisVertical className="size-4 inline" /> icon button in the _"Consumable Notes"_ table and select _"Consume note with …"_ to consume the note with your wallet.
A _"Confirm Transaction"_ modal from Bread Wallet will ask you to sign the consume transaction, which moves the tokens into your account's vault.
