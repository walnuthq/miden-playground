To complete the transfer, the output note has to be consumed by its target account.

On <Link href={`/accounts/${props.walletB?.identifier}`}>Wallet B</Link>'s page, take a look at the _"Consumable Notes"_ section: the **P2ID** output note has been automatically detected as consumable by its recipient.

You'll find a second **P2ID** note there, holding USDCx. Every Transaction pays a fee in the network's fee asset (USDCx on testnet), but Wallet B is brand new and its vault is empty. So the Playground asked the testnet faucet to send it some USDCx.

Click the _"Consume all notes"_ button to create a consume transaction. A single Transaction can consume multiple Notes at once: this one consumes both, and pays its fee out of the USDCx it receives. Consuming the MDN note on its own would fail, since Wallet B would have nothing to pay the fee with.

The preview dialog lets you check the asset changes about to occur, and the fee. Then click _"Submit"_ to complete the transfer.
