Oh no! You lost your phone and your guardian wallet's state is gone forever… or is it?

Luckily, Miden Guardian holds a backup of its latest state: all you need is access to the **Signer**, your Bread Wallet. In real life, this means keeping your Bread Wallet's recovery phrase safe so you can restore it on a new device.

Click the _"Select Wallet"_ button, select _"Bread Wallet"_ and accept the connection request to reconnect it.

Then, on the <Link href="/accounts">accounts page</Link>, click the _"Create new account"_ button and select the _"Restore guardian wallet"_ option.

A modal asks for a name and the address of the guardian wallet to restore. If you didn't save your guardian wallet's address, here it is:

- **Name**: Guardian Wallet <CopyButton content="Copy Account Name" copy="Guardian Wallet" />

- **Address**:

<AccountAddress account={props.account} withLink={false} />

Click _"Restore"_: the Playground fetches the latest state of your guardian wallet from Miden Guardian, including the tokens you received in the previous steps.
