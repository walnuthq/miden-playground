Now that your guardian wallet is deployed, let's fund it by requesting tokens from the [USDCx Faucet](https://faucet.testnet.miden.io/).

Click the _"Mint"_ button to request tokens from the faucet. They are sent directly to your guardian wallet in a public note.

<MintButton />

Once the request completes, wait a few more seconds for the note sent by the USDCx Faucet to be committed on testnet.

The note containing your tokens then automatically appears in the _"Consumable Notes"_ section of your guardian wallet.
