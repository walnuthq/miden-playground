In this tutorial, you'll protect your funds with [Miden Guardian](https://docs.miden.xyz/builder/miden-guardian/), an off-chain service that backs up and syncs private account state without being able to move your funds.

In the previous tutorial, you saw that the state of a **Private** account stays on your device: only a commitment to it is stored on-chain. The flip side is that **losing this state means losing your funds**, just like losing a private key. If your private wallet lives on your phone and you lose your phone, your assets are gone with it.

Miden Guardian solves this by keeping an up-to-date backup of your account state. Every change to the Account must be approved by both you and Miden Guardian, so Miden Guardian always holds the latest state, but it can never move your funds on its own. If you lose your device, you can restore the latest state from Miden Guardian, as long as you still control the Account's signing key. To learn more, read [What is Miden Guardian?](https://www.miden.xyz/blog/what-is-miden-guardian)

Miden Guardian can back up accounts controlled by several signers, but in this tutorial you'll set up a backup for a solo account, controlled by your private Bread Wallet.

Open the Bread Wallet browser extension and select a **Private** account. If you don't have one, click your current account dropdown, click _"Add account"_ and select the _"Off-chain Account (Private)"_ option.

Then click the _"Select Wallet"_ button, select _"Bread Wallet"_, and accept the request to access your account by clicking _"Connect"_.
