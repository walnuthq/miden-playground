Open the {props.account ? <Link href={`/accounts/${props.account.identifier}?tab=guardian`}>Guardian</Link> : <em>"Guardian"</em>} tab of your guardian wallet to list its proposals.

The consume note proposal created in the previous step is in the **Pending** state: it needs your signature before it can be executed.

Click the <EllipsisVertical className="size-4 inline" /> icon button in the _"Proposals"_ table and select _"Sign proposal"_, then approve the _"Opaque signature request"_ prompts in Bread Wallet.

Once signed, the proposal moves from **Pending** to **Ready**. Click the <EllipsisVertical className="size-4 inline" /> icon button again and select _"Execute proposal"_ to execute it with your guardian wallet.

This takes a few moments: the Playground builds the consume transaction with your signature and Miden Guardian's, proves it and submits it to the network. As this is the guardian wallet's first Transaction, it also registers the Account on-chain, and its fee is paid with the USDCx the note carries. Miden Guardian then updates its backup with the new state of your guardian wallet.

Once the Transaction is executed, open the {props.account ? <Link href={`/accounts/${props.account.identifier}`}>Information</Link> : <em>"Information"</em>} tab of your guardian wallet to see your tokens in its asset vault.

When you click _"Next step"_, the Playground simulates losing your device: it disconnects Bread Wallet and deletes the guardian wallet from the Playground.
