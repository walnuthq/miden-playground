In the previous step, you published an increment note on testnet. Your counter contract must now consume it to actually increment its counter.

Open your <Link href={`/accounts/${props.counter.identifier}`}>counter contract's details page</Link>. Under _"Consumable Notes"_, click the <EllipsisVertical className="size-4 inline" /> icon button at the end of your Note's row and select the _"Consume note with …"_ option.

A _"Preview consume transaction"_ dialog shows up with the effects of the Transaction, including the change to the counter's **`count_map`** storage slot. Click the _"Submit"_ button to submit the Transaction to testnet.

Your counter contract uses the **No Auth** authentication component, so you don't need to sign this Transaction. Once it's submitted, the Note is consumed and disappears from the consumable notes.
