Now that we've compiled both our **counter-account** and **counter-note** scripts, it's time to actually deploy our smart contract and interact with it using notes.

From the <Link href="/accounts">accounts page</Link>, click on _"Create new account"_ and select the _"Deploy account"_ option.

Give your new contract a name and select **No Auth** as authentication component and our custom **Counter Contract** account component: our contract doesn't require authentication as we allow any user to interact with it without requiring signature verification.
It means that any user will be able to make the counter account consume the increment note.

When you're done configuring your contract deployment, click on the _"Deploy"_ button to create your account.
